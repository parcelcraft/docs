# Insurance

You can insure any shipment against loss or damage after buying its label.
Insurance costs 1% of the declared value with a $1.00 minimum, and the
quoted **Insurance cost** is shown before you commit. To insure every
label for a flat fee instead, or if labels are already arriving insured,
see [Automatic insurance](#automatic-insurance).

## Add insurance

Insurance is available anywhere you see the **Add insurance** field:

- On the **Print label** tab, right after buying a label. The declared
  value is prefilled from the order total — change it to declare a
  different value, then click **Add insurance**.
- In a bulk batch, each row of the print view has its own insurance
  field, and **Add insurance to all shipments** insures every uninsured
  label in one click, each at its own order total.
- From **Shipment history** — open a shipment and add insurance from the
  manage shipment view.

Insurance can only be added while the shipment is still waiting for the
carrier. Once the package has been scanned or collected, the option
disappears.

An insured shipment shows **Insured for $X** along with the insurance fee
you paid.

## Automatic insurance

EasyPost's **Automatic shipping insurance** add-on insures every label
bought through your EasyPost account for a flat $1.00 per label, covering
up to $100 of loss or damage regardless of the order value. It's an
EasyPost setting, not a Parcelcraft one, so it covers labels from the app
drawer, 1-click ship, and the full-page app alike. While enrolled, each
label shows **Insured for $X** and **Insurance cost $1.00** the moment
it's purchased, without you clicking **Add insurance**. To insure a
shipment for a different amount, use [Add insurance](#add-insurance)
instead.

Turn it on or off under
[Shipping add-ons](https://app.easypost.com/account/shipping-settings?tab=shipping-add-ons)
in your EasyPost dashboard. The status reads **Enrolled** or
**Not enrolled**, and the change applies to labels you buy from then on.

![Automatic shipping insurance toggle in the EasyPost dashboard](/images/v2/easypost-automatic-shipping-insurance.png)

## File a claim

To make a claim on an insured shipment, open it from **Shipment history**
and click **Claim insurance**, which opens
[EasyPost's claim form](https://app.easypost.com/claim-form) in a new tab.
