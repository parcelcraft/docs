# Insurance

You can insure any shipment against loss or damage after buying its label.
Insurance costs 1% of the declared value with a $1.00 minimum, and the
quoted **Insurance cost** is shown before you commit.

## Add insurance

Insurance is available anywhere you see the **Add insurance** field:

- **On the Print label tab**, right after buying a label. The declared
  value is prefilled from the order total — adjust it if needed and click
  **Add insurance**.
- **In a bulk batch**, each row of the print view has its own insurance
  field, and **Add insurance to all shipments** insures every uninsured
  label in one click, each at its own order total.
- **From Shipment history** — open a shipment and add insurance from the
  manage shipment view.

Insurance can only be added while the shipment is still waiting for the
carrier. Once the package has been scanned or collected, the option
disappears.

An insured shipment shows **Insured for $X** along with the insurance fee
you paid.

## Automatic insurance

Automatic shipping insurance is an EasyPost add-on, not a Parcelcraft
setting. When it's on, EasyPost insures every label purchased through your
account for $100 at $1.00 per label — including every label you buy in
Parcelcraft, whether from the app drawer, 1-click ship, or the full-page
app. When it's off, labels are uninsured unless you click **Add insurance**
on a shipment.

To change it:

1. Open [Shipping add-ons](https://app.easypost.com/account/shipping-settings?tab=shipping-add-ons)
   in your EasyPost dashboard.
2. Find **Automatic shipping insurance** and set the toggle. The status
   reads **Enrolled** when it's on and **Not enrolled** when it's off.

![Automatic shipping insurance toggle in the EasyPost dashboard](/images/v2/easypost-automatic-shipping-insurance.png)

The change applies to labels you buy from then on; labels already purchased
keep whatever coverage they had. Labels bought with automatic insurance on
show **Insured for $50.00** and **Insurance cost $1.00** right after
purchase.

> **Note:** Automatic shipping insurance covers up to $100 per label for
> $1.00 — a fixed amount, not the value of the order. To insure a shipment
> for a different amount, use **Add insurance** on the shipment and enter
> the declared value.

To insure each shipment for its actual order value, leave the add-on off
and use **Add insurance** on the shipment, or **Add insurance to all
shipments** in a [bulk batch](/docs/full-page-app/bulk-shipping).

## File a claim

To make a claim on an insured shipment, open it from **Shipment history**
and click **Claim insurance**, which opens EasyPost's claim form in a new
tab.
