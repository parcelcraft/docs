# Printing a test shipping label

Now that you have linked your EasyPost account to Parcelcraft and set up your
origin address in the previous steps, you are ready to print a test label.
Test labels are free — no postage is charged — and confirm that your
integration is working end to end before you buy real postage. Once you can
print a test label, continue to the next section to enable 1-click shipping.

## Step 1: Open Parcelcraft

Log in to your [Stripe](https://dashboard.stripe.com/dashboard) account and
open the [full-page app](/docs/full-page-app): in the left sidebar, click
**Apps**, then **Parcelcraft Shipping** — or jump straight there with
[this link](https://dashboard.stripe.com/app/com.productivity.parcelcraft).
You can also use the drawer by clicking the Parcelcraft icon in the app tray
at the top right of the dashboard; the steps are the same in both.

## Step 2: Start a blank shipment

Click the **+ Create shipment** button at the top of the page. This opens the
[Create shipment view](/docs/full-page-app/create-shipment) with a blank shipment
prefilled from your Parcelcraft defaults.

1. Enter a recipient name and address — any valid address works for a test.
2. Select a carrier and service.
3. Enter a package weight, and choose your packaging.

![Configure a shipment](/images/v2/create-shipment-configure-single-shipment.png)

## Step 3: Print the test label

Click **Print test** at the bottom right of the view. The **Print label** tab
opens showing the test label, the recipient, and a tracking preview:

![A test label ready to print](/images/v2/create-shipment-print-shipment.png)

A test tracking notification is also emailed to your origin address so you
can preview exactly what your customers will receive.

Click **Print Label** to open the label in a new browser tab, choose
**4 x 6" label** (thermal printers) or **8.5 x 11" label** (standard paper),
and click **Print**:

![The print page with label preview and options](/images/v2/create-shipment-print-shipment-step-2-print-pdf.png)

> **Tip:** If the test label doesn't open or print as expected, double-check
> your printer settings and ensure that your printer is connected and
> functioning properly. You can also review the settings on the **Printers &
> digital scale** tab of the Parcelcraft Settings page — including PrintNode,
> which sends labels directly to your printer without a browser print dialog.

By successfully printing a test shipping label, you can confirm that your
EasyPost integration is working as intended and that you are ready to start
processing real shipments through Parcelcraft. Please continue to the next
section to enable 1-click shipping.

If you encounter any issues or have questions during this process, don't
hesitate to reach out to [support@parcelcraft.com](mailto:support@parcelcraft.com)
for assistance. Our support team is available to help you troubleshoot any problems and
ensure a smooth experience with the platform.
